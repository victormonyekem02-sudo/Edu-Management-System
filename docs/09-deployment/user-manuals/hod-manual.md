# Head of Department Manual

As Head of Department (HoD) you check every mark in your department before students can see it. Log in (option `1`); new notifications such as "A mark for Neo Mohapi on 'Algebra homework 1' (MAT101) awaits moderation." are shown first. Then you see:

```
1. Marks awaiting moderation
2. Approve a mark
3. Adjust a mark
4. Moderated marks ready for release
5. Release moderated marks for an assignment
6. Audit trail of one submission
7. Department mark audit log
8. View all my notifications
9. Change my password
0. Log out
```

## 1 - Marks awaiting moderation
Lists every marked submission in your department: submission ID, course, assignment (ID and title), student, raw mark, late deduction, final mark / maximum, and the lecturer who marked it.

## 2 - Approve a mark
Type the submission ID and, optionally, a note (e.g. "Consistent with memo"). The mark becomes *Moderated*. It is **still hidden** from the student.

## 3 - Adjust a mark
If you disagree with the final mark, type the submission ID, the new final mark and a **reason** (required). The lecturer's raw mark is kept for reference; the audit log records the old and new values. You can adjust a moderated mark again as long as it is not released.

## 4 and 5 - Release marks
Option 4 shows moderated marks that are ready. Option 5 asks for the assignment ID and a confirmation (`y`). All moderated marks of that assignment are released at once, and each student is notified. The system tells you how many marks are still awaiting moderation or not yet marked; those stay hidden until you moderate and release them later.

**Released marks cannot be changed.** Check carefully before releasing.

## 6 and 7 - Audit trail
Shows every change to a mark: time, submission, who changed it, the action (MARK, REMARK, MODERATION_APPROVE, MODERATION_ADJUST, RELEASE), old mark, new mark and reason. Use it to answer questions from students, parents or the principal. The audit log cannot be edited by anyone.

You can only see and moderate work from courses in **your own department**.
